## Strength — bench 120 kg on 29 December

Squat 160 · Deadlift 190 (goal 200) · Bench: **he states 110 kg is comfortable**
(27 Sep), against 112.5 on record. Long-term 125+.

pressing exposures. **Keep that principle — do not stack pressing.** A third
weekly bench exposure has been raised with him and is unanswered (B23); it is
not to be added unilaterally.

- **Ladder, re-based v1.26 on a true 110 kg max**, re-verified 19 Oct:
  **105 rung (wk 6) → 112.5 ATTEMPT (wk 7, Tue 6 Oct) → 107.5 rung (wk 10) →
  115 ATTEMPT (wk 11, Tue 3 Nov) → 112.5 rung (wk 14) → wk 15 build week →
  117.5 rung (wk 18) → 120 ATTEMPT (wk 19, Tue 29 Dec 2026).**
  Three attempts, which is the A7 ceiling; every one stands on a trained single
